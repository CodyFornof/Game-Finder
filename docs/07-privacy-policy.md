GameFinder Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

GameFinder respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how GameFinder handles information when you use the GameFinder mobile application.

Information We Collect

GameFinder does not collect, store, or sell personal information from users.

GameFinder does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Information Used by the App

GameFinder may retrieve sports schedules, game information, and broadcast information from third-party services and APIs in order to provide the app's functionality.

These services may receive technical information necessary to process requests, such as an IP address or other standard connection information. Any information collected by third-party services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Location Information

GameFinder does not collect or store your precise location.

If the app uses location information to provide location-based sports or broadcast information, that information is used only for that feature and is not stored by GameFinder.

Third-Party Services

GameFinder may use third-party APIs and services to obtain sports and broadcast information. These third-party services operate independently and may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

GameFinder does not control the privacy practices of these third-party services.

Data Sharing and Selling

GameFinder does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

GameFinder does not maintain a database of users' personal information.

Children's Privacy

GameFinder does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
